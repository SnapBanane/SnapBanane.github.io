About

I'm Julius Gerhardus, a student in Germany, and I've led our school's robotics team, BohleBots, for three years. We started in RoboCup Junior and now play in the Small Size League, where the robots are faster and the software does far more of the work.

Most of my time goes into strategy code and the tools around it. I also write firmware in C++, build things in Python, and keep a few side projects going when the robots are charging. Away from the robots, I spent several years in my school's film club and now run sound as head sound engineer of the school's stage crew.
