    About

    I'm a student in Germany and a developer on BohleBots. We started in RoboCup Junior and now play in the Small Size League, where the robots are faster and the software does far more of the work.

    Most of my time goes into strategy code and the tools around it. I also write firmware in C++, build things in Python, and keep a few side projects going when the robots are charging.
